Our Story

The taste your grandmother made —
kept alive.

Before sugar came in white packets, before biscuits came wrapped in plastic, every Tamil home had a kitchen that smelled of karupatti melting slowly in a heavy pan. That aroma wasn't manufactured. It was earned — through patience, through care, through hands that knew exactly what they were doing.

That tradition didn't disappear because it was wrong. It disappeared because it was inconvenient. We chose inconvenience.

"In a home kitchen in Coimbatore, she rolls each ball by hand. She uses karupatti she chose herself. She ground the millets that morning. The ghee on the pan was made in her own kitchen. She doesn't start until she gets your order. This is Karupatti & Co."

We grind our millets at home. We render our own ghee. We sweeten only with palm jaggery. Your order starts our kitchen — not a pre-made batch, not a conveyor belt. Every groundnut ball, every millet biscuit, every carrot cake was made for you, after you asked for it.

We believe the food you feed your family deserves the same honesty your grandmother gave. And your children deserve to grow up knowing what real food actually tastes like.
